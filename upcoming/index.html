Internet Presence

Tue Nov 10 & Tue Nov 17 · 6 – 9 PM EST · Online

(Vibe)Coding your Artist Portfolio Website

Is your arts practice invisible to the world, or visible only through Instagram posts? In this two-part online workshop you'll build your own artist portfolio website — learning HTML, CSS and JavaScript as the building blocks of the internet, developing locally in an IDE, using vibecoding to articulate your code in natural language, getting it online with GitHub, setting up a custom domain, and making your site findable by the right people. You'll leave with a fully realised site with home, about and portfolio pages, the skills to keep building on it, and feedback from teachers and peers.

No coding experience required
